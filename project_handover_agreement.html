3. Scope of Deliverables & Functional Specifications

A. Core Loan Management Engine (58-Installment Model)

  • Fixed 8% Interest Calculation: Deterministic computation of standard loan terms based on principal and fixed interest.
  • 58-Day Lifecycle: Automated generation of 58 scheduled daily installment records upon loan issuance.
  • Loan Top-Up & Adjustment: Administrative capability to add top-up amounts to active accounts with real-time recalculation of remaining balance.
  • Status Lifecycle: Real-time state transitions across ACTIVE, OVERDUE, PENALTY, and COMPLETED.

B. Autonomous 58-Cycle Compounding Penalty Engine

  • Automated Penalty Application: Zero manual intervention required; system automatically applies an 8% overdue surcharge for every 58 installments past due.
  • Compounding Cycle Engine: Handles prolonged arrears (Cycle 1 at 58 days, Cycle 2 at 116 days, Cycle 3 at 174 days, etc.).
  • Accurate Recalculation: 8% is applied strictly to the remaining outstanding balance at that cycle and added to the principal balance.
  • Audit Logging: Logs every penalty event, cycle count, and arrears timestamp.

C. Field Collections Queue & Scheduling

  • Scheduled For Today: Instant view of all accounts due today, showing today's installment plus accumulated arrears.
  • Consolidated Overdue Queue: Single record per borrower showing days late, missed installment count, overdue start date, and total amount to pay to date.
  • 58-Day Limit Exceeded Queue: Isolates defaulted and penalized accounts for recovery action.
  • Timezone Standardization: Strictly synchronized to Sri Lanka Time (Asia/Colombo, UTC+5:30).

D. Payments & 58mm Thermal POS Receipts

  • Waterfall Repayment Distribution: Automatically settles oldest unpaid installments first.
  • Pre-filled Arrears Settlement: One-click "Collect & Print" pre-fills full arrears while supporting custom partial payments.
  • Direct Thermal Printing: Hardware-exact 384-dot image export for 57mm roll printers, plus browser AirPrint scaling.
  • Itemized Transparency: Displays collector name, payment breakdown, penalty surcharge, and clean date-only next due date.

E. Reporting & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

  • Daily Cash Ledger: Expected collections, actual collected cash, and agent performance.
  • Smart TV Wall Monitor: Auto-refreshing fullscreen dashboard for office displays.
  • User Management: Owner-only permissions to create collector accounts and manage access.
